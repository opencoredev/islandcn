![The Battery preset's charging pill: "Charging" in white, and 42% with a green battery, at real size.](/presets/battery.png)

A battery for a device or accessory, shown as the system's charging indicator: one row, the label on the left and the level on the right. Plug in and the pill appears, its battery fills to the level, it holds for about two seconds and it goes. Expanding it opens the same row with one grey line under the label, not a card.

## Install

```package-install
npx shadcn@latest add {{registry}}/battery-activity.json
```

This writes `components/islandcn/battery-activity.tsx`, plus the content kit and the engine if the project lacks them. Mount the island once first, see [Installation](/installation).

## What it shows

- **Appearing:** the battery's charge fills in from empty over a second as the pill opens. With `dismissAfter` the pill leaves by itself that long after it appears, and comes back each time `charging` turns on again. While the island is open it waits, and leaves a beat after it closes. Reduced motion shows the level at once.
- **Compact:** the pill takes the widest sides the engine allows, 253 pt (the charging indicator is about 317). On the left, "Charging" (or "Charged" when full) in 15 pt semibold white, 14.5 pt in from the pill's end; unplugged, the device's `name` instead. On the right, the percentage at the same size and weight, then a 35.5 pt battery: a solid rounded body with the charge filled from the left, the empty part and the nub darker, and no bolt. While charging the percentage is green and the fill runs mint to lime. Red below `lowThreshold`, white otherwise. The label stays white in every state. If the percentage runs wide in a fallback font, the battery narrows before the spacing does.
- **Expanded:** a long press, `expand()` or `alertOnStart` opens the same row in the shortest expanded island the engine draws (84 pt tall), with one line under the label in 13 pt grey: "Full in 55 min" while charging below full with `timeToFull`; otherwise the device's `name` while charging or full, "Low battery" below `lowThreshold` and "Not charging" when unplugged. A battery with the default name that is full, or charging with no `timeToFull`, has nothing to add and shows the label alone ("Charged" already says it is full). The two lines and the battery are centred on the island's middle, both ends 18.5 pt in, clear of the island's content clip. No large number and no bar.
- **Split:** the battery alone in the 62.18 wide capsule, its fill the level. When the pair is too wide for the split, the fallback is the battery and the percentage.
- **Updates:** the percentage changes its digits in place without a jump in width.
- **Screen readers** hear the name, the percentage and the state, with the time until full while charging when you pass `timeToFull`.

## Usage

```tsx components/headphones-battery.tsx
"use client";

import { BatteryActivity, chargingIndicatorHold } from "@/components/islandcn/battery-activity";

export const HeadphonesBattery = ({ level, charging }: { level: number; charging: boolean }) => (
  <BatteryActivity
    name="Headphones"
    level={level}
    charging={charging}
    timeToFull={charging ? 35 * 60_000 : undefined}
    dismissAfter={chargingIndicatorHold}
  />
);
```

Keep it mounted while the device is connected: it shows when it mounts and each time `charging` turns on, then leaves after `chargingIndicatorHold` (3.3 s). Without `dismissAfter` it stays until you unmount it; a battery that sits in the island all day is noise.
